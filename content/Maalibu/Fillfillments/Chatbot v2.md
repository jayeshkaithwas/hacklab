This document is a setup guide for CloseBot V2, an AI-powered tool for nurturing leads through text messages. Here is a summary of the key points:

**What is CloseBot?** CloseBot is an AI tool that communicates with your client's leads via text, working in conjunction with your call center team to make them more efficient. It is trained on the specifics of your client and industry to respond to leads 24/7.

**How it works** When a lead texts, the call center team handles the calling, while CloseBot manages the texting part of the lead nurturing process. It also integrates with GoHighLevel (GHL) for automated workflow nurture sequences and can immediately respond to inbound messages from leads.

**Setup Process** The setup involves:

- Signing up for a free account on the CloseBot website.
- Migrating from a V1 account to V2, if applicable.
- Choosing a plan, with the "Business plan" being the recommended option for most users at $64/month plus messaging fees.

**Navigating the Software** The CloseBot software is divided into several main sections:

- **Dashboard:** To track metrics like messages sent and bookings.
- **Agents:** To create and set up your AI bot.
- **Sources:** To connect your client's GHL subaccounts.
- **Knowledge Library:** To upload information for your AI, such as Google Docs or scraped websites.
- **Conversations:** To see all the conversations your AI is having with leads.

**Configuration**

- **Personas:** You can set up your bot's personality, including its name, tone, and rules on how it should behave in conversations.
- **Job Flows:** This is where you create a step-by-step process for your bot to follow to accomplish its objectives. This includes providing important business information and details about the offers. The Job Flow builder in V2 is internal to CloseBot and is universal across all sub-accounts.

**Knowledge Library and Follow-Ups**

- The
    **Knowledge Library** is where you can put resources for the bot, like pricing sheets, scripts, and objection handling frameworks.
- You can enable
    **Follow-Ups** in the job flow settings to have the bot automatically follow up with leads who do not respond to its messages.

**Connecting to GHL** Connecting your client's GHL sub-accounts is a simple process that takes only a few clicks per client.
